Subscriptions & Billing

Warvideo Pro is an auto-renewable monthly subscription. Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds are handled by Apple. To manage or cancel, open System Settings › your Apple Account › Subscriptions, or visit Apple’s billing support.

If Warvideo Pro does not unlock after a purchase, open Warvideo and choose Restore Purchases. If it still does not unlock, email us at maliakcadag@gmail.com.

Recording Permissions

Warvideo needs macOS Screen Recording permission to capture video, and optionally Microphone permission for voice. The first time you start a recording, macOS asks for Screen Recording access. If a recording will not start, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording, enable Warvideo, then start the recording again.

Frequently Asked Questions

The recording button does nothing. macOS Screen Recording permission is most likely not granted yet. Enable Warvideo under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen Recording, then tap the record button again.

Where are my recordings saved? Recordings are stored locally on your Mac. You can change the folder in the app’s settings.

How do I cancel? Manage the subscription under your Apple Account in System Settings; cancelling stops future renewals.
