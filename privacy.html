Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Overview

Warvideo is a Mac recording app for gameplay sessions. Recordings are created on your Mac and are stored in the location you choose or in the app's local storage. Warvideo does not upload your recordings to a Warvideo server.

Purchases

Warvideo Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple manages payment, renewal, cancellation, and refund workflows. The app uses Apple's StoreKit services to check subscription status.

Diagnostics

The App Store build may use limited diagnostics to help understand crashes and app reliability. Diagnostics are used for support and maintenance, not for advertising or tracking.

Screen, Audio, and Microphone Access

Warvideo requests macOS permissions needed to record gameplay, system audio, and microphone input when you enable those features. Captured video and audio remain on your Mac unless you choose to share or upload them elsewhere.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the Warvideo Discord support server: Open Warvideo Discord.
